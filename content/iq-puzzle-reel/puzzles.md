# Puzzle bank

Every puzzle has three parts: the question, the obvious wrong try, and the trick. The trick breaks a rule of the page: it moves the question's words, uses the game's own buttons, turns a thing upside down, or takes the question literally.

## Rules for new puzzles

- The question is 5 words or less, read in 1 second.
- The wrong try has to be what most people would do first.
- The trick works on a phone screen: tap, drag, flip, rotate, pinch, or the game's own buttons.
- Animals, kids in danger, injuries: keep it cartoon and gentle. Nobody gets hurt.
